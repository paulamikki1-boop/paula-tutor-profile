Questions

How do I pay?

After I confirm the quote on WhatsApp, I send you the payment details. You only pay once you have agreed the price.

Can I send handwritten answers?

Yes. Take clear, well-lit photos of each page (or scan to PDF) and attach them in the WhatsApp chat.

How quickly will I get it back?

Within 48 hours of payment. Before exam season it is worth sending work early.

What will the feedback look like?

Your work comes back annotated with the marks awarded, where marks were lost and how to fix it. For essays you also get a rewritten paragraph showing what a top-band answer looks like.

Where can I find questions to practise?

Past papers are on the Cambridge and Edexcel websites. The model answers in my Economics notes are a good place to see the standard first.
