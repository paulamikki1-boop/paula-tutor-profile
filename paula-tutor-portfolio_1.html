About Me
Cambridge-trained.
Results-proven.

Hi, I'm Paula — a passionate IGCSE and A-Level tutor based in Kuala Lumpur. I know exactly what it takes to excel in Cambridge exams because I've sat them myself, scoring 9A* in IGCSE and 3A* in A-Levels.

I graduated with a Bachelor's Degree in Biotechnology and have been teaching since 2021. My lessons are tailored to each student — whether you need help understanding a tricky topic, or you want intensive past-year paper practice before your Oct/Nov or May/June exams.

I teach both online and in-person in KL, with flexible 1–2 hour sessions designed around your schedule. I also provide notes and study resources so you always have the right materials to revise with.
